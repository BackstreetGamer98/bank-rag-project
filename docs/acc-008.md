## Savings account opening
The minimum opening deposit for a savings account is INR 1,000. The required average monthly balance is INR 5,000 at all branches. If the balance falls short, the charge is 5% of the shortfall, capped at INR 300 a month.

## Closing an account
Close an account on the customer's written request within 7 working days. There is no charge if the account is closed within 14 days of opening. After 14 days and within 12 months of opening, the closure charge is INR 500.

## Minors
A minor aged 10 or above may run a savings account on their own, with a limit of INR 10,000 a month. For a minor under 10, the guardian operates the account.
