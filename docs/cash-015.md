## Cash limits
A branch vault may hold up to INR 2 crore. A teller counter may hold up to INR 5 lakh. Send any excess to the currency chest by the end of the day.

## PAN requirement
A PAN or Form 60 is mandatory for cash deposits or withdrawals of INR 50,000 or more in a day.

## Dual control
Two custodians must be present whenever the vault is opened.

## Counterfeit notes
Impound counterfeit notes and give the customer a receipt. If 5 or more counterfeit notes are found in a single deposit, file a police complaint within 24 hours and inform FRM.
