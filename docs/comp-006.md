## Acknowledgement
Acknowledge every complaint within 1 working day and give the customer a reference number.

## Resolution time
Resolve a complaint within 30 calendar days. If it needs more time, send the customer an interim update by day 15.

## Escalation
If a complaint is still open after 30 days, escalate it to the Nodal Officer. The next level is the Principal Nodal Officer. The customer may also approach the Banking Ombudsman once 30 days have passed.

## Recording
Log every complaint, including verbal ones, in the Complaint Register on the same day it is received.
