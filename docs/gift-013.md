## Gifts
Decline any gift worth more than INR 3,000 from a customer or vendor, or declare it to Compliance within 5 working days. Always decline cash gifts.

## Entertainment
Meals and events are allowed up to INR 5,000 per person per event with your manager's approval, and no more than 3 per vendor in a year.

## Conflicts of interest
Declare any business a family member has with the bank, and do not take part in decisions about it.

## Outside employment
Outside employment needs written approval from the HR Head.
