## Required documents
Individuals must give one proof of identity (Passport, Voter ID, Driving Licence or Aadhaar) and one proof of address. The address proof must be dated within the last 3 months unless the identity document already shows the current address. A PAN or Form 60 is mandatory for every account.

## Verification
Staff must see the original documents and keep a self-attested copy. Video KYC is allowed for savings accounts only. Video KYC sessions must be recorded and stored for 5 years.

## Periodic refresh
KYC must be refreshed every 2 years for high-risk customers, every 8 years for medium-risk customers and every 10 years for low-risk customers.

## Politically exposed persons
A customer identified as a politically exposed person (PEP) is high-risk. The Regional Compliance Head must approve the account before it is opened.
