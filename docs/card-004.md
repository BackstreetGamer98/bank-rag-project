## Blocking a card
Block a lost or stolen card immediately once the customer's identity is verified by OTP or security questions. Card blocking is available 24x7 through the call centre. A blocked card cannot be unblocked; the bank issues a replacement instead.

## Replacement
A replacement debit card is delivered within 7 working days. The replacement fee is INR 200 plus taxes. The fee is waived when the block is due to fraud.

## Customer liability for unauthorised transactions
If the customer reports an unauthorised transaction within 3 working days, the customer's liability is zero. If it is reported within 4 to 7 working days, liability is limited to INR 10,000 or the transaction value, whichever is lower. After 7 working days, the Disputes team decides case by case.
