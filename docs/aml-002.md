## Raising an internal alert
Any employee who suspects a transaction is linked to money laundering must raise an internal alert to the Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO) within 24 hours. The MLRO decides whether to file a suspicious transaction report (STR) with the regulator, and files it within 7 days of confirming the suspicion.

## Tipping off
Staff must never tell a customer that an alert has been raised or that a report has been filed. Doing so is a disciplinary offence.

## Cash transaction reports
The Compliance team reports cash transactions totalling INR 10 lakh or more in a month across a customer's accounts to the regulator by the 15th of the following month.

## Structuring
Several cash deposits just below INR 50,000 within 7 days may be an attempt to avoid the PAN requirement. Treat this as possible structuring and escalate it to the MLRO.
