## Classification
Customer data is Confidential. Account numbers, Aadhaar numbers and PAN are Restricted.

## Sharing Restricted data
Never send Restricted data through personal email, WhatsApp or any other messaging app. Use approved encrypted email only, and show only the last 4 digits of any number.

## Customer requests for their own data
Answer a customer's request for a copy of their own data within 15 working days, after verifying their identity.

## Data breaches
Report a suspected data breach to Information Security within 2 hours of discovering it. Information Security decides within 6 hours whether the regulator must be told.
