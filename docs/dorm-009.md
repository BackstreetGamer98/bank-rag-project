## Definitions
A savings account is flagged dormant after 12 months with no customer-initiated transaction. It becomes inoperative after 24 months with no customer-initiated transaction.

## Reactivation
To reactivate an account the customer must update their KYC and make a written request. Reactivation is free and is completed within 3 working days.

## Charges
The bank must not charge any penalty because an account is dormant or inoperative.

## Unclaimed balances
A balance that stays unclaimed for 10 years is transferred to the regulator's depositor education fund.
