## Eligibility
Staff in eligible roles may work remotely up to 2 days a week with their manager's approval.

## Devices
Use only a bank-issued laptop. Never store customer data on personal devices or USB drives.

## Network
Connect through the bank VPN at all times. Public Wi-Fi may be used only when the VPN is on.

## Screen and calls
The screen must lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Do not take customer calls in public places.

## AI tools
Never enter customer data into public AI tools or chatbots. Only the bank-approved internal assistant may be used with bank information.
