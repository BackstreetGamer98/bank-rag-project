## Retention periods
Keep account opening forms and KYC records for 5 years after the account is closed. Keep transaction records for 10 years. Keep complaint files for 5 years after the complaint is closed. Keep call recordings for 12 months. Keep CCTV footage for 90 days, or 180 days in fraud cases.

## Destruction
Destroying records needs the Records Officer's approval and a destruction certificate. Shredding logs are kept for 3 years.

## Legal hold
Never destroy records that are under legal hold or investigation, whatever their retention period says.
