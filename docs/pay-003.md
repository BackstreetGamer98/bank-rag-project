## Default daily limits
The default daily limit is INR 5 lakh for NEFT and RTGS combined and INR 1 lakh for UPI. Branch staff cannot raise a customer's limit. The customer applies on the Limit Change form and the Branch Manager can approve a limit of up to INR 10 lakh.

## New beneficiaries
A newly added beneficiary can receive at most INR 50,000 in the first 24 hours after being added.

## Maker-checker
Every branch-initiated payment above INR 1 lakh needs maker-checker approval. The person who starts the payment cannot approve it.

## Failed transfers
A failed transfer is reversed automatically within 3 working days. If the money has not come back after 3 working days, raise a ticket with Payments Operations.
