## Reporting suspected fraud
Report any suspected fraud to Fraud Risk Management (FRM) within 4 hours, using the fraud hotline or the FRM portal.

## Freezing accounts
Freeze or hold an account only on FRM's instruction. The one exception is a customer-reported unauthorised transaction, where staff may place a temporary debit block while they contact FRM.

## Preserving evidence
Save CCTV footage and system logs. In fraud cases CCTV footage must be kept for at least 180 days.

## Police reports
FRM reports frauds above INR 1 lakh to the police. Branch staff do not contact the police themselves unless there is an immediate threat to safety.
