## Raising a dispute
A dispute can be raised up to 90 days after the transaction date. Staff log it in the Dispute Portal with the correct reason code.

## Provisional credit
For unauthorised card transactions up to INR 25,000, the bank gives provisional credit within 10 working days of the complaint. Final resolution must follow within 90 days.

## Chargebacks
File the chargeback with the card network within 30 days of accepting the dispute. Attach the customer's signed form and the statement. For merchant disputes, also attach proof that the customer tried to resolve it with the merchant.

## Failed ATM withdrawals
If an ATM withdrawal fails but the account is debited, the amount is reversed automatically within 5 working days. After that the bank pays compensation of INR 100 for each day of delay.
