## Eligibility
The applicant must be aged 21 to 60 at the time of application, earn a net monthly income of at least INR 25,000, have at least 1 year in the current job and at least 2 years of total work experience, and have a credit score of 700 or above.

## Loan amount and tenure
The maximum loan is 15 times net monthly income, capped at INR 25 lakh without collateral. The tenure can be 12 to 60 months.

## Approval authority
A Branch Manager approves loans up to INR 5 lakh. The Regional Credit Head approves loans above INR 5 lakh and up to INR 15 lakh. The Credit Committee approves loans above INR 15 lakh.

## Documents
Collect the last 3 months of salary slips, 6 months of bank statements, completed KYC and Form 16.
